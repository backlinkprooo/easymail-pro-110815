# EasyMail Pro

EasyMail Pro is a free, privacy-first desktop email client for Windows, macOS, and Linux. Manage Gmail, Outlook, and IMAP accounts in one unified inbox with fast offline search, smart email tools, and optional AI-powered assistance.

## About

EasyMail Pro is a free, modern desktop email client designed to make managing multiple email accounts simple, fast, and private. Available for Windows, macOS, and Linux, it brings Gmail, Outlook, iCloud, Yahoo, and other IMAP email accounts together in a single, intuitive interface. Whether you're managing personal correspondence, business communications, or several inboxes, EasyMail Pro helps you stay organized without unnecessary complexity.
